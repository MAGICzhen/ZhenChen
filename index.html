About

Zhen Chen

I am currently a Postdoctoral Associate in the Miller Lab in the Picower Institute for Learning and Memory at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. I received my PhD in Brain and Cognitive Sciences from University of Rochester in 2023. My advisors were Krishnan Padmanabhan and Ralf Haefner.

My research interests include computational neuroscience, systems neuroscience, dynamical systems, working memory, and cognition. My work focuses on studying computational and systems neuroscience. My work investigates how neural circuits support cognition, working memory, and flexible behavior. I use mathematical analysis, computational modeling, dynamical-systems approaches, and biologically realistic neural-network models to study neural computation.
